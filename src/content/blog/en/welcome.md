I built this site to collect my research, publications and writing in one place. It is generated with [Astro](https://astro.build), hosted on GitHub Pages and available in Turkish and English.

## Where are the articles?

I keep publishing on [Medium](https://medium.com/@isakulaksiz.ce); the blog page lists my Medium articles automatically. This section is only for short notes specific to this site.

The [C Examples](https://corneklerim.blogspot.com) site I built as an undergraduate to share C programming exercises lives in the archive section of the blog page.

## What I will share

- Notes on large language models, RAG and agentic systems
- Natural language processing and machine learning experiments
- Short summaries of papers I read
